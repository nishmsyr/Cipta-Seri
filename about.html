About Us

A dedicated team that delivers quality on time.

Cipta Seri Resources was founded by a group of contracting leads and custom carpenters who wanted to change how home construction and renovations are delivered. We focus on transparency, standard-compliant works, and clear scheduling.

Our Story

Founded on-site, not in an office.

Cipta Seri Resources started in Klang, Selangor, when our founders, then working as independent builders and carpenters, recognized a need for direct, high-quality residential services: house building, renovations, custom cabinetry, curtains, M&E utilities, and professional painting.

So we built a company focused on direct craft delivery — one dedicated team, clear pricing, and a commitment to completing every project close to the date and budget we originally quoted across Selangor.

Mission & Approach

Plan hard, build once.

Most renovation delays come from decisions made too late — a material out of stock, a measurement missed, a permit not filed. We front-load that work. Before a hammer touches a wall, you'll have a full drawing set, a materials list, and a week-by-week schedule.

That's not the most exciting part of renovation, but it's the reason our projects finish close to the date we first quoted.

How We Work

Five stages, start to handover.

Every project — big or small — moves through the same sequence, so you always know what comes next.

01

Site Visit & Brief

We walk the space with you, measure, and note down what the finished space needs to do — for your household or business.

02

Design & Quotation

Layout drawings, material options and a line-item quotation, reviewed with you before anything is confirmed.

03

Scheduling & Permits

We lock a week-by-week build schedule and handle any renovation permits or building approvals needed.

04

Construction & Site Checks

Our crew builds in sequence — structural work, M&E, finishes — with a supervisor checking quality at every stage.

05

Handover

A final walkthrough together, a punch-list clear-out, and a maintenance note for anything that needs care later.
